## Why the bound improves

The original centered definitions use

    P=h^-1*W1, H=h^-2*W2, chi=p2/dstar, u=chi*q,
    F=q*P, G=q^2*H, nu=1+t0^2+2q^2,
    v=a*nu=kappa+2*a*q^2,
    K=(-b*F+a*(G-q^2*psi/2))/v.

On every original nonflat cutoff branch, **2<=v<=3**. The exact centered phase equation is

    2*pi*phi=psi+4*K,
    psi_Z=-4*nu*K_Z/(1+t^2).

Thus the original fixed-phase primitive derivatives become

    A_Z=a_Z*(phi-psi/(2*pi))/2+v*K_Z/(pi*(1+t^2)),
    M=2*B/E=-b*(phi-psi/(2*pi))-a*F/pi,
    M_Z=-b_Z*(phi-psi/(2*pi))-(a*F)_Z/pi
        +2*v*t*K_Z/(pi*(1+t^2)),
    B_Z=(E_Z*M+E*M_Z)/2.

The separate nu_Z*K terms cancel before upper bounds are formed. The accepted backend preserves all slow/inverse terms, the signed small-u/Poisson estimates and the original negative/transition/flat cutoff union. Linear q_Z and p2_Z remain present.
