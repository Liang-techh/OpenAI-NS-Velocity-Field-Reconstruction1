## Physical coordinates and constant viscosity

The same locator solves lambda^2-(z^2/nu)*lambda^(2*delta)=tau, with R=(x^2+y^2)/(2*nu*lambda^2) and Z=z/(sqrt(nu)*lambda^(1-delta)). The actual directed log(lambda) enclosure is retained separately from requested log(tau); lambda is not replaced by sqrt(tau).

For any positive constant nu, the actual source dilation is u_nu(x,t)=sqrt(nu)*u_1(x/sqrt(nu),t) and p_nu(x,t)=nu*p_1(x/sqrt(nu),t). Spatial derivative order m contributes nu^((1-m)/2) for velocity and nu^((2-m)/2) for pressure. First time derivatives at fixed physical position retain sqrt(nu) and nu respectively. This bridge does not support spatially or temporally variable viscosity.

For generic native rows the lambda/nu logarithmic prefactor is transported from its checked log(tau)/2 bound to actual log(lambda). Core rows use their original native norm, amplitude and scale-key factorization directly. The original signed payload remains present beside the transported physical bounds.
