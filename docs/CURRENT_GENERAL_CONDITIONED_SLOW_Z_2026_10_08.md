## Implemented capability

It accepts defining a,b,p2,E functions and their ordinary Z jets in one context, factor basis and ledger. p1 affects the caller's cone certification but not these primitive formulas. E=Utheta/Pstar gives B_over_Pstar and its ordinary derivative; no additional Pstar division is introduced.

The backend restores t0=-b/a, t0_Z=-b_Z/a+b*a_Z/a², kappa=a+b²/a, kappa_Z=a_Z+2*b*b_Z/a-b²*a_Z/a². It differentiates the original q=sqrt((2*eta-(kappa-2))/(2*a))*sigma(1-(kappa-2)/eta), retaining both sigma' and the square-root derivative. Original eta and d_star are frozen positive constants. The live C0 q and differentiated root*sigma record must agree exactly; an equivalent differently rescaled record may conservatively reject and needs explicit refinement rather than a value substitution.

The q_Z=0 shortcut is used only when the source predicate proves the exact flat branch. Active/flat crossing boxes and unresolved signed geometry reject for subdivision. Active smooth-cutoff derivatives use the original directed sigma jets, not a selected cutoff midpoint.

The two signed Möbius angle charts preserve positive rho, s=1/(1+u²) and inverse-h as original source factors, including extremely large |u|. The analytic small-r Fourier branch uses48 terms with directed tails and remains regular at r=0. Four exact midplane identities for J1, J2_osc and their Z derivatives supplement the nonzero-r proof.

The fixed-phase inverse derivative is psi_Z=(2*pi*phi*nu_Z-T2_Z)/(1+t²). Its numerator is evaluated by the exact source identity

    4*pi*(A/a)*nu_Z - 2*t0_Z*J1 - 2*t0*J1_Z - J2_osc_Z

where T1=t0*psi+J1 and T2=t0²*psi+2*t0*J1+2*q²*psi+J2_osc. Cancelling the secular terms symbolically retains tiny source sectors instead of subtracting large independently rounded terms. A_Z=(a_Z/a)*A-a*psi_Z/(4*pi). B_Z uses the complete E_Z,t0_Z,A_Z,a_Z,J1_Z and (t-t0)*psi_Z product rule; the b_Z contribution is retained through the exact b=-a*t0 relation. Derivatives belong to the defining functions at held phase, not derivatives of inverse interval selectors.
