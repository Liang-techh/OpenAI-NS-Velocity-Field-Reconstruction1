# Native Q4 lower substitution witness — 2026-10-31

A new verifier checks whether an already-held live Q4 owner actually defines its first-micro initial R Q_y rows by substitution of its own finite-boundary rows at the chart's lower coordinate. It reads the two referenced substitution nodes directly in that live graph; it does not construct the owner, invoke an ancestor, traverse the graph or evaluate a function.

For ordinary Z0 and Z1, the verifier requires the initial and boundary handles, coordinate and lower handle to belong to the same Q4 graph. It checks the initial node's operation, boundary expression, coordinate variable, lower value and Z-independent-substitution flag. It separately checks the source-owned lower product sc * 1/2, the exact rational half, the sc source-parameter metadata and family, and the sc Z-independence marker. Missing or inconsistent evidence stays unresolved. Passing these checks would establish only the native graph's defining substitution for that particular live owner; it would not supply a numerical flux or bind it to a corrected owner.

## Actual and synthetic evidence

After the input hashes were frozen and an exclusive entry recorded, the synthetic fixture passed all 55 checks. These include wrong expression/variable/value, graph and chart identity, missing or mismatched sc metadata, incorrect lower product or half, and ordinary Z0/Z1. This validates the checker on synthetic live-shaped objects; no real Q4 provider was called.

The same single-entry run authenticated the raw SHA-256 of the earlier original_native_lower_physical_flux_identity execution report and read three prior metadata gates: native initial ports and common prefix; product and Z-independent lower pullback; and the source-owned flat first-micro collar. All three previously saved gates are true. This reuses prior evidence without rerunning its graph computation. It does not prove the present verifier has inspected a real live Q4, nor that the archived graph is identical to a later live graph. The pinned compressed Q4 and prior bundle receipts were hashed as raw bytes and not decoded.

The native initial [129567,129568] and corrected lower [53450,53462] remain separate source ports. A later new source consumer must establish shared physical U, m, raw U_y, R, Pstar, delta, N, predecessor history and ordinary Z0/Z1 defining functions before joining them. The Q4 normalized radial V_y requires one Pstar; corrected packet actual_background_source.V_y is already physical. Identical interval endpoints, names, graph labels or source families are not a substitute for this bridge.

The 212 terms, six signed core/lower contributions, negative complete-pulse target and all retained errors remain unchanged. No signed terminal matching, error cancellation, global five-moment closure or physical NS validation is claimed.
