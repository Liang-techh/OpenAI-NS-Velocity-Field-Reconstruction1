## Leading temporal obstruction and consequence

The checked nonsingular axis source has exactly one axial remainder contribution at the physical origin. Its coefficient has a strictly positive directed lower bound k_lower, its original epsilon/F0/radial logs are zero, its viscosity exponent is1/2, and its lambda exponent is -3+delta<0. At the fixed physical origin, rho=Z=0 and lambda=sqrt(tau). Therefore the present leading family has

`E_z(0,tau,nu)=sqrt(nu)*k_axis*tau^((-3+delta)/2)`.

For0<tau<1 it is bounded below by `sqrt(nu)*k_lower*tau^(gamma_upper/2)`, with gamma_upper<0. Thus this unchanged leading remainder grows as tau approaches0; it cannot have flat terminal-time decay. The producer and independent checker retain and replay the actual source row and sign/scaling evidence.

This conclusion is limited to the present fixed leading family and its current T/E decomposition. It does not rule out the intended higher-order or oscillatory corrected construction. Future flatness work must first cancel or absorb this term using the actual paper recovery/stress corrections. Do not try to prove terminal flatness of this unchanged leading E by accumulating more fixed-time spatial checks.
