# Matching scale evolution to momentum

The current reference velocity is unchanged by the time-tangent patches.
Those patches optimize an independently adjustable time derivative and
pressure. A lower momentum residual obtained that way need not improve
scale recursion. The drift-1800 candidate demonstrates the tradeoff: its
independent peak residual worsens at both tested times.

## Equation to optimize

Let G be the solenoidal physical-time scale generator implemented in
`scale_transport_generator.py`. At the reference time, use zero force as
an explicit diagnostic convention and form

`R(u,p) = G(u) + Du*u - nu*lap(u) + grad(p)`.

The current pressure is held fixed for the first generator-defect replay.
Next, project that residual onto gradients of the existing two compact
pressure bases. This measures their correction capacity. A residual left
after this finite-dimensional projection is not proof that every possible
pressure fails: the basis is limited, and no continuum Helmholtz projection
has been performed. Sampled least squares also does not control the maximum.

If pressure is insufficient, change the reference velocity through compact
curl potentials. The derivative for a perturbation v and pressure q is

`L(v,q) = G(v) + Du*v + Dv*u - nu*lap(v) + grad(q)`.

Because G is linear, the exact nonlinear remainder for amplitude a is
`a*a*Dv*v`. This response is implemented in
`scale_generator_linearization.py`; it accepts analytic spatial derivatives
and the same angular mean projection used by G. It is a reusable operator,
not a fitted candidate or an independently verified field correction.
An optimization using it must retain divergence-free support, moments,
cone conditions and shape behavior, then replay the nonlinear field on an
independent grid and a nontrivial range of scales.

## First actual generator-defect result

`scale_generator_momentum_defect.json` uses 18,720 independent points in
the inner wave domain, volume 2.1361344835903807e-8, at tau0. Actual
five-point Cartesian jets with the current pressure and zero force give:

| Time derivative | Momentum maximum | Volume L2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fitted drift-1800 tangent | 8.91217834095e10 | 1.68732457378e6 |
| Prescribed solenoidal scale generator | 2.66873978670e11 | 2.92939503183e6 |

Replacing only the time derivative increases L2 by 73.61%. The L2 norm
of the derivative change is 2.68561668993e6, whereas the generator itself
has L2 norm 1861.83950585. Thus the fitted dynamical cancellation relies
on a much larger time derivative than this prescribed scale family.
This is not a proof that a corrected reference field cannot recur; it
identifies why the present fixed-reference time-tangent fitting is inadequate.
The reusable NPZ is ignored by Git and regenerated by the adjacent script.
This grid does not certify exterior matching or whole-space momentum.

The completed `scale_generator_pressure_projection.json` fits all 90
pressure columns from the two existing patches (numerical rank 90).
L2 falls only from 2929395.0318 to 2927449.1592, a 0.0664% reduction;
maximum falls from 2.6687397867e11 to 2.6517716920e11 (0.636%). Weighted
projection orthogonality is 1.82e-16 relative to the weighted right-hand
side. This finite pressure basis cannot remove the dominant defect.
The next experiment changes the reference velocity through the linearized
operator above, with a 1% reference-velocity L2 trust bound and exact
quadratic residual replay. Its shape and moment gates still need evaluation
before any corrected field can be adopted.

## Relation to the reference paper

The [OpenAI paper](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/32d9f210-8b73-45e0-91bc-82a30aef8a9a/navier-stokes.pdf),
Section 2 (printed page 3), describes oscillatory momentum flux correcting
the residual of a collapsing background. It also explains why simply
defining a force from the residual does not establish its required smooth
extension through the critical time. This motivates changing the spatial
velocity profile when its scale-compatible momentum cannot be balanced.

Our zero-force projection is a diagnostic choice, not a claim that the
paper uses zero forcing or that an anisotropic scale map is an NS symmetry.
The eventual forced construction must state the force independently and
establish the required temporal regularity, spatial support and energy
properties. No accepted recursive dynamical step exists yet.
