## Source-reference formula

For each side, the packet retains the old C3 refs \(A_i,B_i,C_i,D_i\), \(i=0,1\), corresponding to \(R^2Q,R^2Q^2,R^2U^2,RQ_y\). The identifiers are opaque source references, not numbers. Put \(L=1-\delta Z^2\) and

\[
K=\frac{A_0}{2L}+\frac{B_0}{4}-\frac{Z^2C_0}{L^2}-D_0.
\]

The fixed-geometry candidate `K1_fixed` uses the saved Z1 rows and \(L_{Z,\mathrm{fixed}}=-2\delta Z\). If the source rows are indeed the selected field derivatives under compatible endpoint substitution and \(R>0,L\ne0\), then the general ordinary-Z1 candidate is

\[
\begin{aligned}
K_{Z,\mathrm{full}}={}&K_{Z,\mathrm{fixed}}
 +\frac{R_Z}{R}\left(\frac{A_0}{L}+\frac{B_0}{2}-\frac{2Z^2C_0}{L^2}-D_0\right)\\
&+\delta_Z\left(\frac{Z^2A_0}{2L^2}-\frac{2Z^4C_0}{L^3}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

The two added terms remain symbolic and independently sourced. The packet preserves the old C3 source-reference provenance, but does not claim those references have already been related to a new dynamic-radius source or current F. Source differentiability, capture-safe substitution, common coordinate and physical units, and true \(R_Z/R\) and \(\delta_Z\) bindings remain obligations. No old error is discharged.
