# Q4 lower live-object bundle — 2026-10-31

A new source-only adapter accepts an already-held native Q4 owner and exposes its first-micro lower inputs as their original live references. It does not construct an owner, call an ancestor, evaluate graph handles, select an interval point or perform cross-owner cancellation. The adapter has only synthetic validation so far; it has not been applied to a real Q4 object.

The bundle retains Q4 physical U, m, U_y and Q_y, the finite R Q_y boundary and initial R Q_y ordinary Z0/Z1 handles, chart coordinate/domain/radius/modified fields, delta, Pstar, N, and the loop's incoming and history objects. It checks Q4/loop graph and chart object identities when those references are available, and reports missing fields explicitly. Saved optional error fields remain references; their presence does not certify that all inherited errors were recovered.

An attached reference to the chart's lower coordinate is not sufficient to prove the initial flux was actually formed by substitution there. The adapter therefore leaves that substitution binding unresolved even if the references coincide. It also leaves native/corrected C1 source identity unresolved: same object labels, same source family, same graph owner or overlapping enclosures cannot establish that two distinct source ports are one function.

The earlier static source chain shows Q4 forms U_y by applying Pstar to its normalized radial V_y, whereas the corrected lower packet exposes already physical raw background V_y. A genuine bridge must establish equality of those defining source functions and their ordinary Z1, alongside common U, m, R, delta, N and the original predecessor history. It must connect native initial [129567,129568] to corrected lower [53450,53462] before contracting the endpoint defect.

## Validation and limits

The new, frozen one-entry fixture passed 21 synthetic checks, including object-reference preservation, missing source/history, different graph or chart objects, ordinary Z1, and rejection of an unsupported lower-substitution claim. No real Q4 or corrected source object was evaluated, and no graph expression was traversed or compared in the fixture. This is an implementation interface, not an achieved endpoint identity or a numerical Navier–Stokes result.
