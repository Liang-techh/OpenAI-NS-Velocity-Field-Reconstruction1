# ST073 patch-wide wave-amplitude/pressure screen

Run `python experiments/root_st073/curl_wave_patch_collocation.py` from the repository root. The machine-readable output is `experiments/root_st073/compact_potential/curl_wave_patch_collocation.json`.

The previous Taylor step cancelled the two linear wave harmonics at one cylindrical point, but failed immediately at nearby points. This experiment fits a quadratic tensor-product polynomial for each harmonic's **vector-potential time slope** and pressure over a 5 x 5 spatial patch. The 16 interlaced held-out nodes are not used in the fit. The velocity correction is the curl of the compact potential multiplied by `(tau-tau0)` and a short time taper, so it is exactly solenoidal. It is only a local endpoint Taylor jet, not an integration of the paper's amplitude/phase system.

At `tau=0.0078125`, the amplitude `0.1` wave raised the held-out sampled full-momentum maximum from the unperturbed baseline `728,781` to `10,215,704`. The patch fit lowered it to `3,226,316`, and lowered held-out RMS from `3,395,339` to `978,798`. This is a real improvement over the uncorrected wave but **still worsens the original field**, and remains far above the `1e-3` acceptance target. At amplitude `1`, the nonlinear wave interaction dominates: the held-out maximum remains `144,939,603` after fitting. The fitted linear harmonic RMS fell from `19,323,651` to `4,682,089` on held-out nodes. Direct finite-difference evaluation of the callable corrected field at one held-out point matched the algebraic residual prediction to `0.00092` absolute against a residual near `1e6`.

This evidence changes the next action: a single-point amplitude correction is insufficient, but a supported patch correction can reduce linear harmonic error. The residual left by nonlinear self-interaction and by the axisymmetric mean now dominates. A next candidate must solve the phase/amplitude/pressure evolution through time, add the paper's compact mean correction with the required radial moment conditions, and screen the **full** momentum on the whole support and across time. The current correction is rejected; it does not establish a critical-time Navier--Stokes solution.
