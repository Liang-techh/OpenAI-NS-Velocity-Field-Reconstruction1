# Candidate-specific exact-curl wave re-entry

The `annular_pressure_scale_screen` candidate can now be passed directly to `radial_peak_cone.py` and `curl_wave_prototype.py`. The source generator unwraps the compact core through experimental field wrappers and accepts an interior `tau`. The wave uses the **actual selected integer angular modes**, enforces distinct modes so their cross terms vanish in the angular mean, and uses the correct backward-time transport sign: the physical operator is `-∂τ + u·∇`, hence the frozen phase coefficient is `∂τΦ=u·k`. An optional fifth-order time bump is supported wholly inside the registered slab.

At `r=0.0056890761915166545`, `z=0.0034`, the candidate's local cone passes at `tau=0.0081`, `0.0084`, and `0.0087`. At `tau=0.0084`, two distinct modes `m=1,4` give an exact positive local stress representation after periodicization and transverse projection. This is a frozen pointwise covariance fit, not an amplitude solution. The same axial line loses the cone by `z=0.0035`, motivating an axial halfwidth of `5e-5` for a wave centered at `z=0.0034`; a uniform cone pass over even that narrow support has not been shown.

With radial halfwidth `7e-4`, axial halfwidth `5e-5`, and time halfwidth `3e-4` centered at `tau=0.0084`, the prototype is a spatial exact curl with smooth compact time support. Its 16-angle center sample matches the target covariance to numerical precision, yet the **sampled finite-difference complete physical momentum maximum rises from `4.57e5` to `2.70e9`**. Halving the spatial and temporal steps twice changes the wave maximum from `2.699e9` to `2.703e9`, while the finite-difference divergence shrinks from `18.4` to `0.075`, consistent with an exact curl and a severely underresolved narrow envelope. It is rejected.

The physical scale audit explains the failure. Over a 5×5 interior envelope grid, the exact-curl envelope remainder exceeds the principal carrier by up to `529` for `m=1` and `175` for `m=4`. To bring that ratio below one by increasing integer frequency requires multipliers at least `529` and `175`. At the previous `2.5e-6` time step, the lenient viscous damping exponent bound `ν|k|²Δτ≤1` permits multipliers only up to `21` and `6`. Even at the minimum remainder multipliers, that bound requires steps below approximately `4.1e-9` and `3.3e-9`. These are dimensional screening estimates, not the [OpenAI paper's](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/32d9f210-8b73-45e0-91bc-82a30aef8a9a/navier-stokes.pdf) normalized theorem. Its amplitude equation (7.5) retains pressure and viscous damping, while (7.38) controls the remainder generated by taking the curl. The current frozen Kelvin amplitude and cutoff do neither.

This result changes the next construction step: widen the admissible radial-axial-time cone region or redesign the slow envelope and phase together before attempting another high-frequency pulse. Then solve the damped transverse amplitude-pressure equation and retain all exact-curl, cutoff, mean, and nonlinear terms in the physical residual. A center covariance fit alone does not indicate progress toward the `1e-3` momentum target.
