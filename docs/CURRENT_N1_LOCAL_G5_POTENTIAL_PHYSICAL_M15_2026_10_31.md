```
-2 Lambda^-2 integral_0^1 eta(q) rho(q) Flead(q,Z) F1(q,Z) d rho.
```

The original velocity cutoff has exact mass `integral eta dq=5/8`; for `0<hb<1/2`, `rho²` lies in `[16,16 exp(sc/4)]`. Hence the coefficient-only physical factor is `-5 sc [1,exp(sc/4)]`, with formal shift `(hb,F0_anchor,Pstar_squared,Lambda) += (1,0,0,-2)`. The physical measure is applied once.
