## Exact source and derivative adapters

The original source p2_Z sum contains both Pstar sectors and one common radial factor. When their huge absolute logR upper bounds are indistinguishable at finite precision, a generic addition can select an absolute upper-log reference and lose the common R factor. ```text


No native scale, datum or coefficient is selected as a new field value. The original coefficient/error record and the newly correlated carrier enclosure are retained together.
