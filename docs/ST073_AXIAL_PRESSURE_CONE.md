# Axial pressure shape screen for the local wave cone

The fixed-radius wave-cone failure in the axial collar is largely a target-stress orientation problem. At `tau = 0.5/64` and `r = 0.0056890761915166545`, the unmodified field passes only 3 of 13 axial nodes between `z = 0.0025` and `z = 0.003432627453438968`.

`experiments/root_st073/axial_pressure_cone_screen.py` adds four independent, compact pressure shapes in the existing axial collar. Their radial factor is `(1-(r/rsupp)^2)^5`; their axial factors are `256 s^4(1-s)^4 P_n(2s-1)` for Legendre degrees `n=0,1,2,3`, where `s=(|z|-zflat)/(zsupp-zflat)`. The velocity is unchanged, so its divergence and finite-slab support properties are unchanged. The pressure and its first spatial derivatives vanish at the collar boundaries.

The four coefficients are selected by a linear cone-feasibility step and a constrained least-squares residual step. Requiring cone ratio below `0.8` succeeds at all 13 fitting nodes. At the 12 intervening axial midpoints, which are not fitted, all 12 also pass; the largest midpoint cone ratio is `0.7934`. The sampled residual RMS on fitting quadrature rises from `183,876` to `187,160` (about 1.8%), and the maximum center residual rises from `737,608` to `745,529`. The registered amplitudes are stored in `experiments/root_st073/compact_potential/axial_pressure_cone_screen.json`; `load_candidate()` exposes the field for subsequent experiments.

This establishes a wider local axial cone candidate than the previous coefficient rescaling. It is **not** an accepted Navier–Stokes construction: these are one-time, one-radius finite samples; pressure-only fitting does not ensure the global pressure-Poisson relation; the full residual remains orders of magnitude above `1e-3`; and the exact-curl wave, radial neighborhood, and time evolution still need to be constructed and checked. The next useful step is to couple these pressure shapes to a compatible solenoidal transition correction and test a genuine radial-axial-time patch.
