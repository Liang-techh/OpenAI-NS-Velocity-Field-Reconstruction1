## Preserve giant-radius correlations

The original pulse unit contains exp(−(.5+mu)t), while radial velocity also contains sqrt(R/2), R=Rp exp(t). Enclosing the opposite t/2 terms separately can give a huge false upper bound when mu is extremely small. The new adapter combines them analytically:

Pstar exp(−(.5+mu)t) sqrt(R/2) = Pstar sqrt(Rp/2) exp(−mu*t).

The other velocity units use the same exact change of source-log bases. mu*t is evaluated as xi on main/exit/gap, as 13+mu*s on gap-end/end, and as mu*t on entrance. Twenty exact factor identities retain all radial differentiation orders through four. The full native derivative grids are unchanged; fixed basepoint units are not differentiated again. Original radius, Cartesian and fixed-position time operators remain unchanged.
