# Joint wave polarization and growth candidate

`wave_stress_growth_codesign.py/.json` jointly fits 27 complex potential
coefficients to the ten radial-flux components at five wave-region nodes.
The targets are those of the saved 44-control constrained mean. Complete
exact-curl covariance forms are used, with a positive-growth constraint in
mass-whitened coordinates and an explicit coefficient search bound.

Three deterministic SLSQP starts produced feasible fitted candidates. The
final selected candidate has relative five-node flux mismatch 4.69e-11
and assembly growth lambda 1000.000000000669. Direct angular covariance
replay agrees with the fitted fluxes. On higher quadrature, lambda is
997.3601 (energy rate 1994.7202): positive, but below the imposed lambda
floor of 1000. The numerical floor is therefore not independently verified.
This is not a spatial stress-matching certificate away from the five nodes.

The candidate is large. Physical wave RMS is 2321.36 versus mean RMS
533.98 on the same patch, a ratio of 4.347. All wave quadratic products
must be retained; a small-perturbation approximation is inappropriate.
Original and whitened matrices, flux forms and selected coefficients are
saved to support subsequent joint residual fits without repeating assembly.

The first limited-time-direction physical replay ran while the codesign
source was being finalized. Its source coefficient snapshot was not saved;
`codesigned_mean_wave_replay.json` is explicitly marked as a preliminary
diagnostic that cannot reconstruct the final candidate. Do not use its
metrics as the final candidate's validation. The replay loader now requires
an embedded immutable wave snapshot for codesigned inputs, and new runs
save that snapshot.

Next evaluate the locked final candidate with all potential time-derivative
directions and pressure harmonics 0, 1 and 2. This retains the nonlinear
mean and second harmonic generated by the actual wave. Any improvement
still needs independent full Cartesian momentum replay and renewed mean
compatibility; instantaneous covariance/growth fitting is not a trajectory,
PDE solution, or scale recursion.
