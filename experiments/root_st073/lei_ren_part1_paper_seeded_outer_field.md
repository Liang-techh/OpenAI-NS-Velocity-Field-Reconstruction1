Run `python experiments/root_st073/lei_ren_part1_paper_seeded_outer_field.py`.
Use `SeededOuterField(build_joined_field()).physical_field().velocity(x,y,z,t)`
for the installed candidate. The same inner field, angular schedule and axis
pressure are retained. The pulse and both end bumps use the Rh-seeded solve.
Before Rp the mean retains the old exterior integral plus the actual inner
mass offset; afterwards the new incoming m1 already contains that offset.
The joined radial formula now consumes the seeded mean directly.

Three consistency fixes are required by this installation:

- Incoming stage radii use the schedule Decimal precision. Default 28-digit
  arithmetic previously erased offsets at logRref=5e152, giving wrong Ep.
- End-bump point values and primitives use the same MP quadrature
  normalization, and the point coordinate stays MP through translation.
- After pulse support, its primitive uses the stored full-row input of the
  coefficient solve. Recomputing at another precision leaves a spurious Rv
  endpoint difference. The mean is never assigned zero.

reported Rv mean jump at working precision, and independently sampled pulse
mapped-divergence relative cancellation 3.899e-25 on the finer stencil.

replay is 2.870e-444. These are not full momentum residuals.

cannot solve this conditioning problem. Float-Z derivative uncertainty and
